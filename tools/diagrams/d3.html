User flow — three roles, one shared state

Eight features, used by three kinds of people who never need the same thing. The invariant underneath all of them: every page renders only what was known at its issue time, and city, scenario, clock and selection all travel in the URL — so any screen can be bookmarked, or sent to a colleague exactly as it was.

The invariant that makes the whole thing reviewable

Every page is a pure function of (city, scenario, issue time). Nothing is cached across issue times, nothing is written back, and no page can show a figure another page contradicts — because there is one engine instance and they all read it.

What the flow is missing for real operations

No shift handover, no audit trail of who saw what and when, no acknowledgement that an action was actually ordered, and no multi-user state. Plan comparisons survive one visit. An operations centre needs all four.
