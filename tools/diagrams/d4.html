Inside the engine — how one cascade is forecast and broken

A worked trace of the Nagpur monsoon reconstruction at T+37 h. The same four mechanisms run for every scenario: an ensemble that carries uncertainty, a filter that bends it toward the evidence, a graph learned from the streams, and a counterfactual that re-simulates the city to price an action.

1 · Why 200 futures instead of oneBorrowed from numerical weather prediction.
Each member is a different plausible city
Not noise around one answer. Every member draws its own storm timing and totals, its own drainage and substation capacities, and its own dependency link weights — then the whole city is simulated forward under that draw.

Probability is read from the spread
“A 73% chance Ambazari T-point goes critical” means 146 of 200 members had it cross — not a confidence interval drawn around a point forecast.
Known weakness, measured not hidden
The ensemble is over-dispersed: its 80% band contains the truth 92–93% of the time. It is too humble rather than overconfident — the safer failure, but still a calibration error.

Why localisation matters
A member that misread a pump in the north keeps its credibility about a hospital in the south. Without this, one bad gauge discredits otherwise-correct futures across the whole city and the forecast collapses.
Forgetting λ = 0.985
≈23 h of memory, so the filter tracks a situation that is itself changing rather than anchoring on hour one
Tempering ESS ≥ 12%
If too few members carry real weight, the update is softened — the ensemble can never collapse onto one future
Distrust λ → 0.82
A stream whose trust falls below 0.5 is forgotten three times faster: a frozen gauge stops steering the forecast
3 · The graph, learned not configuredSparse Bayesian learning with ARD.
What is actually inferred
Whether stress at asset j at step k predicts stress at asset i at step k+1, after accounting for the hazard both are under. Irrelevant causes are driven toward zero precision and drop out.

The control that proves it works
Each city's dataset contains seven deliberately fake links that no physical mechanism supports. The learner is scored on whether it prunes them — a configured graph could not be tested this way at all.
Only excess stress travels
An asset operating inside its design limit passes nothing downstream. A substation at 60% does not endanger a hospital; the same substation at 94% does.

The lead time this buys
The hospital is forecast to divert at T+44.5. The forecast is issued at T+37.0 — 7.5 hours of warning on a facility nobody is yet looking at, derived from a lake level that has not overflowed yet.
What a per-system alarm would have seen
Nothing. Every asset in the chain is inside its own threshold at T+37. The drainage desk sees a full lake, the discom sees a healthy yard, the hospital sees normal access. The chain is only visible across all three.

The output nothing else produces: an hour
For this plan — pre-stage ambulances, barrier the Vasant Nagar substation, close Jhansi Rani Square early, fuel the mobile sites — benefit decays as the start slips. Start by T+47.0 h to keep 80% of it; by T+55.0 h it is worth less than half. That is the difference between “act soon” and a decision a duty officer can defend.
Why re-simulation, not a lookup table
An effectiveness rating assumes the action works the same in every event. Re-simulating asks what it does to this event, on the members that match this evidence — which is why the same action scores differently in the cloudburst and in the dam release.
What is deliberately not computed
No money, no vehicle or pump counts, no travel time, no crew locations, no residents-protected figure. The model does not compute them, so the product does not display them. A crew is an abstract unit of capacity, labelled as one.
How it is scored

Replay re-runs the full 72-hour window with the future locked away and grades every forecast against the hidden truth: lead time, probability of detection, false-alarm ratio and reliability. A pretrained foundation model (Chronos-Bolt) is run on identical issue times and facilities as a control — the ensemble wins, mostly on warning lead.

The honest limits

The ensemble and the simulated truth share a model family, so structural error is zero by construction — a real deployment would face model error this test cannot show. The dependency prior is centred near the truth. The browser holds roughly 200–300 facilities before the ensemble gets heavy.
